### Trending News #1: US-China Trade Truce Extended to January 10
- **Source**: CNBC / Investing.com / Nikkei Asia
- **Summary**: Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent confirmed Thursday that the US and China have extended their trade truce by two months, pushing the deadline from November 10 to January 10, as President Xi Jinping began a state visit to Washington for a summit with President Trump. The original truce, struck in Busan, South Korea last October, had Washington lowering tariffs on select Chinese goods while Beijing paused new rare-earth export restrictions, resumed US soybean purchases, and moved to curb fentanyl precursor chemical flows. China has already bought roughly half of its 25-million-ton annual soybean commitment, including one million tons in a single September week. The announcement, made ahead of formal Trump-Xi talks, removes near-term tariff-escalation risk that had weighed on markets, though investors remain focused on whether the summit yields a broader, durable agreement or just another rollover. Asian equities traded mixed as details emerged, with Chinese mainland shares slipping even as Indian and Singaporean markets gained.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High

### Trending News #2: Stitch Fix Plunges 18% on Weak Guidance
- **Source**: Investing.com / Benzinga
- **Summary**: Stitch Fix shares plunged as much as 18% in after-hours trading Wednesday after the online personal-styling company's fiscal 2027 revenue guidance badly missed Wall Street expectations, overshadowing an otherwise better-than-feared fourth quarter. Q4 revenue came in at $324.4 million, roughly in line with estimates, while the company posted a narrower-than-expected loss of $0.02 per share versus a projected $0.06 loss, marking a ninth straight quarter of year-over-year revenue growth. But active clients fell 1.4% both quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year to 2.277 million, underscoring persistent customer attrition. The real shock came from guidance: first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue is expected between $323 million and $328 million, badly trailing the $355.1 million analyst consensus. With shares already near 52-week lows, the disappointing outlook triggered an outsized selloff, reinforcing investor skepticism that Stitch Fix's turnaround efforts can offset shrinking client rolls in a tough retail environment.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: Medium
