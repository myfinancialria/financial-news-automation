### Trending News #1: SK Hynix's Solidigm Weighs $150B Nasdaq IPO
- **Source**: Reuters / Investing.com
- **Summary**: SK Hynix's US-based memory unit Solidigm is exploring an IPO that could value the business at up to $150 billion, in what would become the largest-ever US semiconductor listing. Sources say Solidigm held "bake-off" pitch meetings this week with investment banks competing for lead roles, with a potential listing targeted as early as 2027 and proceeds that could reach $15 billion. The plans remain early-stage and could shift with market conditions. SK Hynix built Solidigm by folding in its $9 billion acquisition of Intel's NAND and SSD business, positioning the unit to capture surging AI-driven demand for enterprise storage hardware. A $150 billion valuation would dwarf recent chip debuts, including Arm's roughly $54 billion 2023 listing and Cerebras Systems' roughly $56 billion valuation earlier this year. If it proceeds, the IPO would underscore how AI infrastructure buildout is reshaping memory and storage economics, and could pressure rivals to accelerate their own AI-storage strategies.

### Trending News #2: Court Rules Against Kalshi in Ohio, Tennessee
- **Source**: CoinDesk / CNBC
- **Summary**: The Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals dealt prediction-market operator Kalshi a fresh legal setback, ruling that its sports-related event contracts are not "swaps" under federal commodities law and therefore remain subject to state gambling regulation. The panel affirmed a lower court's denial of Kalshi's injunction bid in Ohio and vacated a separate order that had blocked enforcement in Tennessee, clearing both states to enforce their sports-betting laws against the platform. The decision deepens a growing circuit split: the Third Circuit sided with Kalshi in New Jersey in April, while the Ninth Circuit backed state authority in Nevada in August. With federal appeals courts now split three ways, legal observers expect the question of who regulates sports prediction contracts — the CFTC or individual states — to ultimately land before the Supreme Court. The ruling adds regulatory uncertainty for Kalshi and similar platforms as they scale nationally, and could slow new state-by-state expansion.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: Medium
