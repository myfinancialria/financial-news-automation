### Trending News #1: Bank of Japan Hikes Rates to 31-Year High
- **Source**: CNBC
- **Summary**: The Bank of Japan raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to 1.25%, the highest level since 1995, capping its two-day policy meeting on Friday. The decision passed on a split 7-2 vote, with two board members dissenting in favor of holding steady, as the central bank flagged mounting risk that inflation could overshoot its 2% target. It's the third hike in the BOJ's tightening cycle that began in March 2024, and the fastest pace yet — this increase came just three months after the last one, versus six months previously. Markets reacted immediately: the yen weakened roughly 0.25% against the dollar even as the 10-year JGB yield rose to 2.019%, its highest since 1999, and the Nikkei 225 gained 1.28% on relief buying in bank stocks. Economists polled by Reuters now see the policy rate reaching 1.5% by March 2027 and 1.75% by mid-2027.
- **Sentiment**: Neutral
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/japan-raises-rates-30-year-high-yen-jgb.html

### Trending News #2: Congress Sends Trump 100% Russia Oil Tariff Bill
- **Source**: CNBC / NBC News
- **Summary**: The U.S. House passed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act 262-159 this week, sending the sweeping sanctions package to President Trump's desk, where it remained pending his signature as of Friday. The bill authorizes tariffs of up to 100% on the top five importers of Russian oil and natural gas — a list topped by China, which buys roughly half of Russia's crude exports, and India, which takes about 37% — alongside sanctions on Russian banks, officials and the "shadow fleet" tankers moving sanctioned oil. It cleared the Senate 86-11 in August with bipartisan support, and 58 House Democrats crossed party lines to back it despite leadership warnings that it hands the White House overly broad tariff authority and could raise U.S. consumer prices. If signed, it would mark one of the most aggressive U.S. trade-and-sanctions escalations against Russia's energy exports since the Ukraine war began, with China and India facing the biggest exposure.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/russia-sanctions-bill-lindsey-graham-congress.html

### Trending News #1: Amazon's $8B Generac Deal Powers AI Boom
- **Source**: CNBC / Yahoo Finance
- **Summary**: Generac shares surged more than 40% in extended trading Wednesday after Amazon signed a landmark supply deal worth up to $8 billion for backup power generators to fuel its data center buildout. Generac issued Amazon warrants to buy 1,693,745 shares at $200.93 each — worth up to $340 million, or roughly 3% of Generac's fully diluted share count. About 308,000 shares vested immediately; the rest vest as Amazon's cumulative payments grow, with rights extending to 2033. Initial deliveries total $2.4 billion across 2027-2028. The deal highlights how AI-driven data center expansion is reshaping industrial supply chains — the IEA says data center energy use jumped 80% from 2020 to 2025. Generac's market cap was near $10.3 billion before the news broke. For investors, it signals hyperscalers increasingly locking in critical power suppliers with equity stakes rather than standard contracts, a trend likely to extend to other generator and grid-infrastructure names.

- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amazon-takes-warrant-stake-generac-123324020.html

### Trending News #1: Fed Hikes Rates to 4%, First Since 2023
- **Source**: CNBC / Yahoo Finance
- **Summary**: The Federal Reserve delivered its first interest rate hike since 2023, lifting the benchmark rate by 25 basis points to a target range of 3.75%-4.00% in a unanimous vote. Chairman Kevin Warsh declared inflation "too high and has been for too long," rebuffing White House pressure to hold rates near 1% and insisting the Fed will "stay in our lane." Markets sold off during his press conference: the Dow dropped over 600 points (1.2%), the S&P 500 fell 0.4%, while the 10-year Treasury yield climbed above 5% and the dollar strengthened. The Fed's updated projections show most officials expect one more quarter-point hike before year-end, with traders pricing roughly 51% odds of an October move. The hike marks a decisive pivot toward inflation-fighting after years of low rates, raising borrowing costs for consumers and businesses and signaling a tougher path ahead for equity valuations and rate-sensitive sectors.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html

### Trending News #2: Meta-Backed Junk Bond Funds AI Data Center
- **Source**: Bloomberg
- **Summary**: CleanSpark is marketing a $2.23 billion junk-bond offering — the first high-yield debt deal directly tied to a Meta data center — underscoring how AI infrastructure spending is reshaping credit markets. The five-year notes, priced via Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and Wells Fargo, carry an expected yield near 8.5%, about two points above typical BB-rated debt, but are backed by a 20-year, $6.6 billion lease in which Meta guarantees rent and operating costs for a Sandersville, Georgia facility due online in Q4 2027. The deal is part of a broader wave: data-center developers have sold roughly $39 billion in high-yield bonds this year, the largest chunk of new US junk-bond supply, as the IEA projects data centers will drive half of new US electricity demand growth. It shows hyperscalers' credit guarantees now let even junk-rated builders borrow cheaply — but also how much leverage underpins the AI buildout.
- **Sentiment**: Neutral
- **Impact Level**: Medium
- **Link**: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/meta-tied-data-center-taps-us-junk-bonds-for-the-first-time

### Trending News #2: SEC Clears Path for Tokenized US Stocks
- **Source**: CNBC / Decrypt
- **Summary**: The SEC opened a regulated path for trading tokenized U.S. stocks, issuing a five-year "Innovation Exemption" that lets approved trading venues and liquidity providers operate without registering as exchanges or dealers under the 1934 Securities Exchange Act. The order covers genuine tokenized shares carrying full dividend and voting rights — not offshore-style synthetics — and gives issuers a 30-day window to object before their stock can be tokenized by a third party. The move, seen as a major step toward 24-hour equity trading on public blockchains, sent shares of tokenization platform Securitize soaring 14.93% to close at $8.93 on the NYSE. It marks one of the clearest regulatory green lights yet for blending traditional securities markets with blockchain infrastructure, and could accelerate similar tokenization bids from brokerages and fintechs racing to capture round-the-clock trading demand from retail and institutional investors alike.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/securitize-jumps-after-regulators-greenlight-tokenized-us-stocks.html
