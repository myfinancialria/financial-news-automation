### Trending News #1: Fed Proposes Stablecoin Reserve, Capital Rules Under GENIUS Act
- **Source**: The Block / CoinDesk / PYMNTS
- **Summary**: The Federal Reserve took a major step toward regulating dollar-backed stablecoins, proposing two rules to implement last year's GENIUS Act. The first requires Fed-supervised issuers to back every stablecoin dollar-for-dollar with permissible reserves — short-term Treasury bills and other high-quality liquid assets — and imposes a sliding capital charge: 2% on the first $20 billion in stablecoins outstanding, stepping down to 1% above $50 billion. Issuers must redeem coins within two business days, and a sustained capital shortfall through a full quarter triggers forced liquidation of reserves. A second proposal creates a tailored application process for board-supervised banks seeking to issue stablecoins, plus formal appeals and hearing procedures. The Board opened a 60-day public comment period once the rules publish in the Federal Register. The move signals regulators shifting from crypto skepticism to active rulemaking, giving banks and issuers a clearer — if costlier — compliance roadmap.

- **Impact Level**: High
