### Trending News #1: Telix Buys Germany's ITM Isotope for $1.65B
- **Source**: Investing.com / Reuters
- **Summary**: Australia's Telix Pharmaceuticals agreed Sunday to acquire Munich-based ITM Isotope Technologies for $1.65 billion in upfront consideration, creating a vertically integrated radiopharmaceutical leader. Telix will pay $1.25 billion in its own shares and assume $302 million of ITM's net debt, plus up to $700 million more tied to regulatory approvals and sales milestones for ITM's lead candidate, ITM-11, which just completed Phase 3 trials for neuroendocrine tumors. ITM shareholders get 105.8 million Telix shares, leaving Telix holders with about 76.3% of the combined company. ITM supplies therapeutic isotopes like lutetium-177 and actinium-225 across 65+ countries, posting $273 million in 2025 revenue on a 40% CAGR since 2021. Combined 2026 revenue is projected above $1.3 billion. The deal closes by year-end, pending shareholder and regulatory sign-off, and cements Telix's control over its isotope supply chain as radiopharma competition intensifies.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High

### Trending News #2: Jefferies Sees S&P 500 Hitting 9,000 by 2027
- **Source**: Investing.com
- **Summary**: Jefferies reaffirmed a bullish call late Sunday, projecting the S&P 500 will reach 9,000 by end-2027 after an 8,000 target for end-2026, betting on continued earnings strength rather than further valuation expansion. The firm forecasts $373 EPS in 2026 (35% growth, above consensus' 29%) and $450 EPS in 2027 (20.8% growth), applying a 20x multiple versus today's 21.5x forward multiple. Roughly 46% of the index has AI or data-center exposure, with those names seeing 60% earnings growth in 2026 before moderating to 24% in 2027. Jefferies flags risks from Treasury yields, up over 60 basis points year-to-date, and rising fiscal deficits and tech debt issuance pressuring long rates — historically a drag on equity multiples. The firm stays overweight technology, financials, healthcare, and materials while cautious on communication services, consumer discretionary, and real estate, signaling Wall Street's earnings-led optimism remains intact heading into 2027.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: Medium
- **Link**: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/jefferies-stays-bullish-on-sp-500-sees-9000-by-end2027-4908198
