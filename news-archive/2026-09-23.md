### Trending News #1: Jana Partners Pushes for Six Flags Sale
- **Source**: Investing.com / WSJ / CNBC
- **Summary**: Activist investor Jana Partners is publicly pressing Six Flags Entertainment to explore a sale, escalating a campaign it began building last October alongside a stake held with NFL star Travis Kelce. Jana holds roughly a 9% position worth about $200 million and, in a letter to the board, argued management should "reverse course" and engage with known buyer interest rather than continue as a standalone company. The firm also called for a change in board leadership, targeting chair Marilyn Spiegel, who has led the board since January. The push comes after a rough stretch for Six Flags: shares are down nearly 20% year-to-date following integration troubles from its 2024 merger with Cedar Fair. A sale process, if launched, could unlock a takeover premium for shareholders, but also signals deeper concerns about the combined company's ability to execute on its own. Investors will watch for the board's response in coming weeks.

- **Impact Level**: Medium

### Trending News #2: Financial Stocks Fall on AI, Yield Curve Fears
- **Source**: Reuters / Investing.com
- **Summary**: Bank and financial shares sold off sharply as investors weighed two separate threats: rising competition from artificial intelligence in wealth management, and a flattening Treasury yield curve that squeezes lender profits. The S&P 500's broader financial index closed down 2% and its bank sub-index fell 3%, with brokerages hit hardest — Charles Schwab dropped 6.1%, Ameriprise fell 4.4%, and Raymond James lost more than 3%. The AI concern centers on Meta's newly launched Muse app, which investors fear could eventually encroach on advisory and wealth-management services traditionally run by banks and brokerages. Separately, the gap between short- and long-term Treasury yields has been narrowing, which erodes banks' net interest margins since they borrow short and lend long. Together, the two pressures triggered one of the sector's worst single-day showings in weeks, even as broader indexes like the Nasdaq notched fresh records on tech strength.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/financial-stocks-fall-with-ai-and-flattening-yield-curve-in-focus-4911692

### Trending News #1: Anthropic in Talks for 1-Gigawatt Data Center Deal
- **Source**: The Information / Investing.com
- **Summary**: AI lab Anthropic is in early talks to lease up to 1 gigawatt of computing capacity directly from Stream Data Centers, a developer majority-owned by Apollo Global Management, according to The Information. The move would make Anthropic a direct tenant rather than relying solely on cloud providers like AWS and Google Cloud, giving it more control over power, space, and hardware guarantees as AI compute demand explodes. The facilities would likely run on Broadcom- and Google-designed tensor processing units, alongside possible Nvidia GPUs. Discussions have also explored having Google provide a credit guarantee to backstop the deal. No agreement is finalized yet, and terms remain fluid. The talks add to Anthropic's broader infrastructure push, which reportedly includes over a dozen letters of intent for data center leases exceeding 1 gigawatt combined, underscoring how compute scarcity is reshaping deal-making across the AI industry.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High

### Trending News #2: Beijing Intervenes to Rescue China Vanke Debt
- **Source**: Reuters / Investing.com
- **Summary**: China Vanke shares jumped sharply after Reuters reported that Chinese financial regulators took fresh steps to shield the distressed property developer from default. Shenzhen-listed shares rose 3.7% to 3.95 yuan while Hong Kong shares climbed 4.6%. Regulators reportedly asked banks not to classify overdue Vanke loans as non-performing, directed lenders to extend repayment deadlines, and requested delays on collecting interest payments. Vanke has been among the few major Chinese developers to avoid an outright default despite the prolonged property market downturn, having previously missed onshore bond repayments in late 2025 before securing bank approval to defer interest. Analysts call this one of Beijing's strongest interventions yet to stem a potential Vanke default. The move signals authorities remain committed to preventing systemic contagion from property-sector distress, even as it raises questions about the health of China's banking sector balance sheets.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High
- **Link**: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/china-vanke-shares-rose-sharply-on-regulatory-debt-intervention-4912048

Bitwise's CIO called it potentially crypto's strongest bull run yet, with capital rotating out of AI stocks and into digital assets.
