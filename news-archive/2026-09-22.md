### Trending News #1: Bitcoin Breaks $86K as Crypto Winter Debate Ends
- **Source**: CNBC
- **Summary**: Bitcoin pushed to $86,349.90 late Monday, its highest level since late January, extending an update on the $85K breakout flagged in the previous briefing. The token last traded near $85,863, up roughly 5.8% on the day, as a short squeeze forced out more than $260 million in bearish bets within a single hour, with total crypto market liquidations reaching about $271.8 million. Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan told CNBC the "crypto winter" — the depressed stretch that followed bitcoin's October 2025 record above $126,000 — is now over, predicting what could become the "strongest and longest-running bull market in crypto's history." Renewed spot ETF demand and regulatory tailwinds are fueling the move. Traders are watching whether spot buyers keep showing up as leverage rebuilds; a sustained hold above $86K opens a path toward $88K-$90K, while a slip back under $80K would undercut the newly bullish narrative.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: High

### Trending News #2: Vicor Surges 12% on AI Power Royalty Deal
- **Source**: GlobeNewswire / Investing.com / Benzinga
- **Summary**: Power technology maker Vicor Corporation jumped about 12.6% in extended trading Monday after sharply raising its third-quarter 2026 revenue guidance, lifting sequential growth expectations from near 10% to more than 20%. The upgrade stems from royalties tied to a newly announced non-exclusive license of Vicor's patented Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) technology, granted to a major AI OEM that can now source modules from Vicor or third-party suppliers while still paying royalties. CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli noted some hyperscalers approached Vicor after their systems hit import bans over infringement of its NBM patents. Four leading OEMs and hyperscalers have now licensed the technology as AI data centers race to solve power-delivery bottlenecks. The move signals Vicor can monetize decades of power-electronics patents through licensing alongside its manufacturing business, positioning it as a picks-and-shovels beneficiary of the broader AI infrastructure buildout.
- **Sentiment**: Bullish
- **Impact Level**: Medium
- **Link**: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/21/3365846/0/en/vicor-corporation-raises-q3-2026-revenue-guidance.html

## 📍 Slot 2: 9:00 AM IST

### Trending News #1: RBA Chief Bullock Signals Imminent Rate Hike
- **Source**: InvestingLive
- **Summary**: Australia's central bank chief just moved markets a week ahead of its next decision. RBA Governor Michele Bullock took part in a fireside chat hosted by CEDA in Sydney at 1:00 PM AEST (0300 GMT), reiterating that the upside inflation risks she flagged in August "are now materialising." She pointed to higher-for-longer oil prices and businesses' growing willingness to pass on cost increases, framing the key question as whether policy is tight enough to bring inflation back to target in a reasonable time. Markets already price roughly 70-75% odds of a 25 basis point hike at the RBA's September 28-29 meeting, which would lift the cash rate above 4.35%. Traders now await Thursday's Australian labour force data for further confirmation. A hawkish surprise would extend AUD strength and pressure rate-sensitive sectors, while any softer tone could quickly unwind the hike bets markets have built up over recent weeks.
- **Sentiment**: Bearish
- **Impact Level**: Medium
- **Link**: https://investinglive.com/central-banks/economic-and-event-calendar-in-asia-tuesday-september-22-2026-rba-governor-bullock-speaking/

### Trending News #2: Iran's Pezeshkian Lands in NY as Trump Warns
- **Source**: CNBC Africa
- **Summary**: Geopolitical risk is back in focus as Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian arrives in New York this week to lead his delegation at the UN General Assembly, running September 22-26 and 28, where he will address the assembly and meet world leaders amid the ongoing U.S.-Israel-Iran conflict. President Trump escalated his rhetoric ahead of the visit, telling Fox News his options include "wiping Iran out" or letting its economy "rot" unless Tehran strikes a deal. Still, Eurasia Group expects Brent to stay elevated near $90-$110 through year-end as Iran-backed proxies keep pressuring shipping lanes, and Iran's military has threatened "painful" retaliation if Washington strikes. Markets will watch closely for any Trump-Pezeshkian contact this week as a signal of de-escalation or a fresh flare-up.
