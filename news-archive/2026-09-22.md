### Trending News #2: UBS Shares Sink Ahead of Capital Rules Vote
- **Source**: Investing.com / Reuters
- **Summary**: UBS shares fell more than 3% Tuesday after CEO Sergio Ermotti rejected a proposed compromise on new Swiss capital rules as inadequate, days before a decisive parliamentary vote. The rules, drafted after Credit Suisse's 2023 collapse, could force UBS to hold up to $20 billion in additional Common Equity Tier 1 capital by requiring the bank to back foreign subsidiaries with as much as 100% CET1, versus Ermotti's preferred 90%. Chairman Colm Kelleher and Ermotti have lobbied lawmakers, warning that even a 50% AT1 compromise would mean finding another $13 billion in capital and would leave UBS at a competitive disadvantage to international rivals. Swiss business lobby groups have echoed the pushback, calling the plan "excessive." The Swiss parliament's upper house votes Wednesday, the last major hurdle before the rules take effect. A harsh outcome could pressure UBS's returns and reshape how the bank structures its global balance sheet for years.
